Privacy Policy

Chrome Extension Privacy Policy

Learn how the EasyData MCP Connector respects your workspace, handles page contexts, and maintains user data safety.

🛡️ Overview & Developer Statement

The EasyData MCP Connector Chrome extension is built to help users seamlessly transfer prompt requests between their favorite AI chatbots (Gemini, Claude) and the EasyData platform. We believe in absolute privacy: the extension operates local context triggers and does not track, store, or sell any of your information.

🔑 Permissions We Require

The extension uses standard permission limits to operate safely in your browser. Below is a detailed breakdown of the permissions requested in our manifest:

  • contextMenus: Enables the context menu item ("Send schema to EasyData") when you highlight text inside chat panels. No page data is read unless you select text and explicitly click this item.
  • Host Permission (https://easydata.is/*): Allows the background worker script to forward your selected text request securely to the EasyData API to provision your database applications.

🛑 Zero Data Collection & Storage

The extension is designed around data minimization. We do not collect, monitor, or store any data. Specifically:

  • No Browsing History: We do not log which pages you visit or analyze your chats.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not store email addresses, names, or account profiles.
  • No Cookies or Third-Party Tracking: The extension operates without trackers, analytical tags, or advertising components.

🔒 Data Transfer & Security

All data sent by the extension is initiated directly by the user. When you explicitly trigger the "Send schema to EasyData" action, the highlighted text is sent via a secure HTTPS connection directly to https://easydata.is (or your configured local server). We implement Transport Layer Security (TLS) to guarantee the confidentiality of your schema transmissions.

⚖️ GDPR and Educational Safety Compliance

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and school data privacy guidelines, the extension contains no telemetry or behavioral analysis. It serves purely as a bridge between the AI interface and the EasyData application engine. Teachers can safely deploy the extension across school Chromebooks and client stations without risking student privacy or storing personal records.

If you have any questions or feedback regarding the extension's operations or privacy guarantees, please reach out to us at privacy@easydata.is.
