# Chrome Web Store: Publisher Contact Email Configuration

This error indicates that you have not configured a verified contact email address for your Developer Account. This is a developer-account-level setting, not a setting within the extension code itself.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Resolution Guide

To add and verify your publisher contact email, follow these steps:

1. **Open the Chrome Developer Dashboard:**
   * Go to [Chrome Web Store Developer Console](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole).

2. **Navigate to Developer Account Settings:**
   * Look at the left-hand sidebar menu.
   * Click on the **Gear icon (Settings)** or **Developer Account** option near the bottom.

3. **Enter the Contact Email:**
   * Under the **Account info** or **Contact Information** section, locate the **Contact email** field.
   * Enter your preferred public support or administrator email address (e.g., `support@yourdomain.com` or your Gmail address).

4. **Verify the Email Address:**
   * After saving the email, Google will send a verification link to that address.
   * Check your inbox for the email from Google Chrome Web Store.
   * Click the verification link to activate it.

5. **Publish the Extension:**
   * Once verified, return to the **Item Edit** page for the Gemini MCP Connector extension.
   * The warning message should be resolved, and you will be able to proceed with publishing.
